The Sovereign Soul Architecture

A complete three-chapter masterclass. Become unstoppable. Claim what is yours.

The Premium Container

Become unstoppable. Claim what is yours. Step into your sovereign power.

This three chapter package offers an intensive comprehensive 12 session course that delves into the deep-rooted subconscious blocks around shadow work, finances and self-worth, and boundary enforcement.

Twelve 75-minute counselling sessions, targeted and structured towards shadow work and supported by somatic work, guided meditation, parts work, EFT (Emotional Freedom Techniques), CIT (Conscious Integration Techniques) and journalling, with take-home integrations to complete.

The Three-Chapter Arc

Three phases of complete transformation

The architecture builds chapter by chapter, first the armour, then the shadow, then the crown.

Chapter One, The Armour & The Boundary

Focuses on dropping masks, and erecting unshakable personal boundaries.

Chapter Two, The Shadow Crucible

Focuses on diving into the subconscious, healing hidden trauma, and reclaiming raw power.

Chapter Three, Radical Worth and Wealth Expansion

Focuses on healing 'lack' mindset, activating luxury codes, and stepping into the spotlight.

The Twelve Sessions

Your masterclass, session by session

Twelve structured 75-minute sessions, grouped into the three phases of the work. Tap each phase to explore the journey.

Focuses on dropping masks, and erecting unshakable personal boundaries.

  1. 01

    Deconstructing the Mask

    Identifying the protective armour - what is the underlying childhood conditioning that you have built to survive life? We unpick these childhood coping mechanisms and how they are showing up in your current day-to-day life.

  2. 02

    The Anatomy of the Velvet Glove

    Learn to move from passive people-pleasing to diplomatic authority, learning how to communicate a "No" clearly, cleanly, and without guilt.

  3. 03

    Erecting a Fortress

    Learn how to map personal, professional, and energetic boundaries and create strategies for enforcing those boundaries.

  4. 04

    Integration & Somatic Grounding

    A session dedicated to integrating and processing the previous 3 sessions of work, and somatic tools to help the nervous system adjust to holding space for personal power.

Focuses on diving into the subconscious, healing hidden trauma, and reclaiming raw power.

  1. 05

    Navigating the Subconscious Trenches

    We use dream analysis and targeted journalling to identify repressed emotions, hidden fears, and unvoiced anger.

  2. 06

    Alchemising Trauma into Power

    We learn how to sit safely with heavy emotional patterns without flinching. Moving from a mindset of survival and victimisation into a state of personal sovereignty.

  3. 07

    The Severing Ritual

    Energetically cutting cords with toxic past dynamics, family entanglements, or relationships that thrive on control and power struggles.

  4. 08

    Mid-point Integration Sanctuary

    In this session you have a safe, highly nurturing space to weep, release, and integrate the intense shadow work before moving into expansion.

Focuses on healing 'lack' mindset, activating luxury codes, and stepping into the spotlight.

  1. 09

    Deconstructing the 'lack' Mindset

    In this session we expose inherited family blocks around money, scarcity, and survival instinct. Re-writing the subconscious script around financial security.

  2. 10

    The Royal Standard of Self-Worth

    This session teaches you how to stop shrinking, claim your expert status, and establish a "premium" standard for your time, energy, and work.

  3. 11

    Visibility and the Magnetic Ask

    Step out of the shadows and onto the stage! In this session we overcome visibility fears, learn how to pitch your premium services, and attract high-value networks.

  4. 12

    Crowned Sovereignty

    The final session is an integration session - fusing shadow, boundary and wealth work into a lifelong spiritual blueprint. We complete the masterclass with a final graduation ritual and your commitment to the sovereign path.
