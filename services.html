Standalone Session

Talking Therapy Session

Honouring your depths. A 60-minute live Zoom session. £70.

True spiritual growth requires us to meet, understand, and integrate the hidden parts of ourselves. This 60-minute live talking therapy session offers a sacred, non-judgemental space, relying on the transformative power of dialogue, conscious exploration, and spiritual presence.

Supported by intuitive spiritual guidance, we will gently unearth unconscious patterns, process emotional blocks, and bring clarity to your soul's journey. To ensure you feel completely held throughout this deep dive, our time together includes guided meditations to connect with your subconscious, followed by dedicated anchoring and grounding techniques so you feel safe and deeply rooted in your body. We will close our session by pulling an oracle card to integrate the underlying energy of our work, leaving you with a clear, resonant message to carry forward. It is a potent, conversational hour designed to help you face your depths, reclaim your personal power, and align with your highest truth.
